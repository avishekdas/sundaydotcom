## Spike verdicts (reviewer)
- **S-1 PASS stands.** All thresholds are met with a margin of 5× or more under the stated setup. The stronger claims (finding 7, D-030) need M0A-9 and M0A-10 before they are relied on.
- **S-2a PASS with change is justified.** Variant A failed and variant B met the threshold. The D-029 reasoning (WAF runs before viewer-request Functions) is consistent with the evidence. Reproducibility (M0A-8) and the doc updates (M0A-7) are still owed.
- **S-3 PASS stands** under the stated setup. The margin is thin for the real package; the re-measure is already planned.
- **S-4 PASS stands on the thresholds**, but it does not show drift-freedom once contributors are removed, and it left out the formula and row-level visibility (M0A-11).

## Verdict
**M0a done after fixes: M0A-1, M0A-2** (both are small changes to `0000_bootstrap`/`0001_init` and their tests; fix them in place before any environment applies these migrations). Should-fix items M0A-3…M0A-13 should be closed or explicitly carried into the M0b plan before M0b's Mutation builder or migrate λ work depends on them.
